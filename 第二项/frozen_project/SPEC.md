# Scientific Specification (Stage 1 frozen)

## Research target
Estimate the relative chemical difficulty of each reaction step generated by AiZynthFinder when exact experimental conditions are unknown.

## Final scientific predictors (exactly three)
1. Feasibility difficulty `D_F = 1 - P_feas`.
2. Precedent difficulty `D_P = 1 - P_prec`.
3. Normalized delta synthetic complexity `D_SC`.

The model does not estimate experimental success probability, reaction yield, optimal reaction conditions, catalyst/solvent/temperature/reaction time, safety, cost, environmental impact, or toxicity.

## Reaction direction
Precursors >> product.

## Planned later-stage outputs
- Reaction difficulty `D_reaction` in [0, 100]
- Uncertainty `U` in [0, 1]
- Confidence: high / medium / low
- Route bottleneck difficulty, mean difficulty, hardest reaction step, and route confidence

Lower difficulty means easier; higher difficulty means more difficult. `P_feas` is a model feasibility probability and must not be called experimental success probability.

## Stage-1 boundary
No reaction-difficulty value is calculated in Stage 1. No formal targets, routes, reaction extraction, predictor calculation, annotation, training, or search-reward changes are permitted.

Baseline: AiZynthFinder 4.4.1. This specification is frozen for subsequent stages; only implementation/version/path notes may be appended without changing the scientific objects, three-factor definition, or stage boundary.
